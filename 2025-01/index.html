Consuming

I finished Cat's Cradle by Kurt Vonnegut.

I kept thinking I would have more to say about this. It was great but I don't think I can draw direct comparisons to anything I've been thinking on. All of it is too direct an analogy to the threat of nuclear destruction to be comparable to any of the issues computing actually threatens (or faces). It's great, and a fantastic piece of almost science fiction (I'd call it sci-fi, the library disagrees). It reads like butter, and I'd recommend it for that reason alone but it also happens to be a fun story with interesting concepts, so I would recommend it.
