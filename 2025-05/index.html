Now - Non-Stop Travel

Posted on May 14, 2025

Considering & Consuming

Traveling a ton: Seattle and Asheville were both great and very different trips.

Seattle, WA

Mostly downtown and along the water with occasional trips to the suburbs. The metro cars were clean, and metro stations were less so. The Mountain and the range were in view the first couple days, and the weather the rest of the time wasn't too bad. Was wishing we had time to get out to see either up close; next time.

Suddenly being a morning person was nice, got work done early and had plenty of time for a run / wandering afterward then even stuff at night after that. I'm sure after too long on that schedule the lack of sleep would catch up with me.

Asheville, NC

Spent most of the time out in the middle of almost nowhere, didn't really make it down town. It was great. Plenty of windy roads up, down and around mountains. Hiking and running in the woods and along the river. Promising progress on recovery from the floods, what wasn't quite built back up didn't get in the way of our good time.
