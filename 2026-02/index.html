Consuming

Spy X Family, Season 3

Apparently I had fallen behind, so I caught up and watched the latest season, fun show that feels increasingly prescient1 these days.

Episode 2 left a huge impact on me, spoilers indented below:

Absolute punch in the gut at the end. The unexpected obliteration of a happy future hit hard. I wasn't looking for a twist, and I was immediately devastated when the turn was taken. If you've been reading about the ongoing senseless genocides and wars, this may hit you hard too. a

Episode 3 went on to be equally if not more heartbreaking. But it kinda took the expected turn and made sense. Cutting the this into two episodes dramatically made the first part better in my opinion, especially watching it with a day in between as I did, the end of part 1 stuck with me.

The consistent call back to episode 2 via the credits at the end of each episode, added to that. It kept the stakes that they're fighting for at top of mind, and the whole arc added a lot of context and motivation to Twilight's character.

The season had an exciting end as well, my main gripe with the show and specifically this season is that Yor could use exploration. There's a lot to her life that is mentioned and implied but we don't see, and I think it could add a lot to the show overall to look into that more deeply.
