Mistborn: The Well of Ascension

The start to this second installment was a bit slow, so this series fell by the wayside. I picked it up again this past week, and once the roller-coaster’s buildup reached it's peak, the ride down was looping, twisting, and exciting.

As much political drama as fantasy, it's story of transitional struggles felt grounded. Instead of vast expansion, we went deeper into this vibrant decaying world. Secrets, obfuscated by the prior thousand year's subjugation, became immediate problems for this newly forming empire.

The mysteries and misdirections that built up throughout the novel left me eager pick up the next, which is already off to a bang.

Title   Mistborn: The Well of Ascension
Author  Brandon Sanderson
